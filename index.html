Delete Your Owlora Account

Owlora: Your Study Companion

How to delete your account

  1. Open Owlora: Your Study Companion.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Want to keep a copy? Before deleting, export a backup in Settings → Backup & Restore.

If you can't sign in, email beetexe.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Owlora Account Deletion" from the email linked to your account. We may ask you to verify that the account is yours.

What happens after you request deletion

Immediately
You're signed out on all devices, your notes, flashcards, quizzes, and study plans are deleted from the phone you used, and the account is scheduled for deletion.
Within 7 days
You can cancel by signing back in with the same Google account. If you have Cloud Sync, your synced content is restored. Otherwise you can restore it from a backup you exported.
After 7 days
The account is permanently deleted and this can't be undone. Signing in later creates a new, empty account.

What data is deleted

  • Synced study content: notes, folders, flashcards, quizzes, and study plans stored through Cloud Sync
  • Profile: name, email address, and profile photo
  • Remaining AI and scan credits, which are forfeited and non-refundable
  • Sign-in sessions on all devices
  • Study content stored on the phone you used to request deletion

What isn't deleted or is kept

  • Your Cloud Sync subscription isn't cancelled automatically. Cancel it in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Content on other phones where you're signed in stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Purchase and credit records are kept but anonymized.
  • Encrypted server backups may still contain your data for up to 30 days after deletion, then they're removed automatically.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their privacy policies.
  • Some limited records may be kept where required by law, or to resolve disputes or prevent fraud.
