Account & privacy

Delete your Owlora account

You can delete your Owlora: Your Study Companion account from the app. If you cannot access the app, you can contact us using the details below.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open Owlora: Your Study Companion.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Review the information shown and confirm your request.

If you cannot sign in or no longer have access to the app, email beetexe.dev@gmail.com with the subject Owlora Account Deletion. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

What data is deleted?

When your request is processed, your Owlora account information and associated data stored on our servers should be deleted, where applicable. This may include your account profile and cloud-synced notes, folders, flashcards, quizzes, study plans, and related records.

Content stored only on your device may not be deleted from that device by deleting your online account. Before proceeding, export or save anything you want to keep. You may need to remove local content separately in the app.

Data that may be retained

Limited records may be retained when required by law or when reasonably necessary to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, or comply with legal obligations. Purchase records held by Google Play or RevenueCat are also subject to their respective policies and retention practices.

Any data retained for these reasons will be kept only for as long as necessary for the applicable purpose.

Need help?

For account deletion assistance, contact beetexe.dev@gmail.com.
