Account & privacy

Delete your Owlora account

You can delete your Owlora: Your Study Companion account directly in the app. If you can't access the app, you can ask us by email instead.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Owlora: Your Study Companion.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Read the confirmation message and tap Delete Account to confirm.

If you can't sign in or no longer have access to the app, email beetexe.dev@gmail.com with the subject Owlora Account Deletion, from the email address linked to your account. We may ask you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

What happens after you request deletion

  • Right awayYou're signed out of Owlora on all your devices, and your account is scheduled for deletion.
  • Within 30 daysChanged your mind? Just sign back in with the same Google account and the deletion is cancelled — everything stays as it was.
  • After 30 daysYour account is permanently deleted, as described below. This can't be undone, and signing in again later creates a brand-new, empty account.

What data is deleted

  • Your synced study content — all notes, folders, flashcards, quizzes, and study plans stored on our servers through Cloud Sync.
  • Your profile — your name, email address, and profile photo are removed from your account.
  • Your credits — any remaining AI and scan credits are forfeited and can't be refunded or transferred.
  • Your sign-in sessions — all sign-ins on every device are ended.

Content on your phone: deleting your account doesn't remove content stored on your device. To remove it, uninstall Owlora or clear the app's data in your phone's settings. If you want to keep anything, save or export it before you delete your account.

Your subscription isn't cancelled automatically. If you subscribe to Cloud Sync, cancel it in the Google Play Store (Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) so you aren't charged again. Deleting your Owlora account alone doesn't stop Google Play billing.

Data that is kept

  • Purchase and credit transaction records are kept for accounting and legal purposes, but they're anonymized — after deletion they're no longer linked to your name or email.
  • Server backups may contain your data for a short period (up to 7 days) before they're automatically overwritten.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat keep their own records of your purchases under their respective privacy policies.

We may also keep limited records where required by law, or where reasonably necessary to resolve disputes or prevent fraud and abuse — only for as long as needed for that purpose.

Need help?

For help with deleting your account, contact beetexe.dev@gmail.com. For more about how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.
